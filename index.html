Flagship Project
Autonomous Agricultural Robot
GPS-enabled robot for automated ploughing, seeding, and fertilizing
+
Problem Statement

Indian agriculture faces a severe labor shortage with rising manual labor costs. Traditional farming methods for ploughing, seeding, and fertilizing are time-intensive, inconsistent in coverage, and economically unsustainable for small-to-mid-scale farmers.

Engineering Approach
  • Designed a GPS-enabled autonomous navigation system with waypoint-based path planning for precise field coverage
  • Integrated multi-sensor fusion (GPS + IMU + ultrasonic) for accurate localization and obstacle detection
  • Engineered modular tool-attachment mechanism for interchangeable ploughing, seeding, and fertilizing implements
  • Developed embedded firmware on microcontroller for real-time motor control and sensor data processing
Technical Solution
  • Custom chassis with differential drive system for terrain adaptability
  • GPS-guided navigation with sub-meter accuracy for row-by-row field coverage
  • PWM-based motor control with encoder feedback for speed regulation across varying soil conditions
  • Autonomous operation mode with manual override capability for safety

Ductile Iron Optimization
Heat treatment analysis of Ferritic grade-400/15 for enhanced mechanical properties
+
Research Objective

Investigate and optimize the mechanical properties of Ferritic grade-400/15 ductile iron through controlled heat treatment processes to determine the most effective thermal cycling parameters for enhanced performance in industrial castings.

Methodology
